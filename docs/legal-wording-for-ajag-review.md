# Statutory Declaration Wording for AJAG Review

Oct 17, 2026 · @Thomas Huet

The CAF Digital Travel Itinerary generates a statutory declaration for each missing receipt, in English or French. The structure follows DAOD 7000-1, but some sentences were written for the tool and are not in any official source. Those are listed first for review; wording copied from official sources is listed after, for reference only.

## Drafted wording to review

These sentences were written for the tool. Square brackets show what the tool fills in from the claim.

| # | Where | English | French | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Declarant paragraph | I, \[full name\], \[PRI\], \[rank in full\], of \[home address\], a member of the Canadian Armed Forces, | Je soussigné(e), \[nom au complet\], \[CIDP\], \[grade au complet\], demeurant au \[adresse\], membre des Forces armées canadiennes, | Adapted from the DAOD 7000-1 form, which reads "a member of the Canadian Forces (if a civilian, delete and insert occupation)". The tool uses "Canadian Armed Forces", adds "of" / "demeurant au", and "(e)" in French. |
| 2 | Declarant paragraph, civilian | a civilian employee of the Department of National Defence, | employé(e) civil(e) du ministère de la Défense nationale, | Drafted. The DAOD says to insert the occupation; the tool uses this fixed wording. |
| 3 | Paragraph 1 | On \[date\], I was on temporary duty in \[location\] to \[purpose\]. | Le \[date\], j'étais en service temporaire à \[lieu\] pour \[objet\]. | Drafted. |
| 4 | Paragraph 2, general expense | During this duty, I paid \[amount and currency in words\] for the following expense: \[description\]. | Pendant ce service, j'ai payé \[montant et devise en toutes lettres\] pour la dépense suivante : \[description\]. | Drafted. |
| 5 | Paragraph 2, lodging | During this duty, I stayed at \[hotel\], \[city\], from \[date\] to \[date\] (\[n\] nights), and paid \[amount\] for lodging. | Pendant ce service, j'ai séjourné à \[hôtel\], \[ville\], du \[date\] au \[date\] (\[n\] nuits), et j'ai payé \[montant\] pour l'hébergement. | Drafted. |
| 6 | Paragraph 2, currency exchange (exchange during the duty) | During this duty, I exchanged \[amount sold\] for \[amount bought\] at \[bank or exchange office\]\[ and paid a service charge of \[amount\]\]. | Pendant ce service, j'ai échangé \[montant vendu\] contre \[montant acheté\] auprès de \[banque ou bureau de change\]\[ et j'ai payé des frais de service de \[montant\]\]. | Drafted. Updated 17 Oct 2026: the place is now the bank or office (the field asks "Where exchanged"), and paragraph 1 takes the duty location from the itinerary for that date. |
| 7 | Paragraph 2, telephone call | During this duty, I paid \[amount\] for a \[business / personal\] telephone call to \[person\] at \[number\]. | Pendant ce service, j'ai payé \[montant\] pour un appel téléphonique \[d'affaires / personnel\] à \[personne\] au \[numéro\]. | Drafted. |
| 8 | Paragraph 3 | The original receipt for this expense is unavailable because: \[reason\]. | Le reçu original de cette dépense n'est pas disponible pour la raison suivante : \[raison\]. | English: wording requested by LCol Huet. French: drafted. |
| 9 | Paragraph 4 | I have not been reimbursed for this expense by any other means, and I will not claim it again. | Je n'ai été remboursé(e) de cette dépense par aucun autre moyen et je ne la réclamerai pas de nouveau. | Drafted. |
| 10 | Matter line (IN THE MATTER OF / EN CE QUI CONCERNE) | Missing \[Expense\] Receipt; Missing Lodging Receipt; Missing Currency Exchange Receipt; Missing Telephone Call Receipt | Reçu manquant (\[dépense\]); Reçu d'hébergement manquant; Reçu de change manquant; Reçu d'appel téléphonique manquant | Drafted. The DAOD leaves the matter blank. |
| 11 | Phrase before the facts, French | (official, see below) | DÉCLARE SOLENNELLEMENT QUE : | From s. 41 of the Loi sur la preuve au Canada. The French DAOD sample uses the affidavit wording "FAIT LE SERMENT OU AFFIRME QUE" even on its declaration form, so the statute's wording was used instead. |
| 12 | Jurat opening | DECLARED before me at the \[place\] in the Province of \[province\], this \_\_\_ day of \_\_\_\_\_\_ A.D. 20\_\_. | DÉCLARÉ devant moi à \[lieu\], dans la province de \[province\], ce \_\_\_ jour de \_\_\_\_\_\_ 20\_\_. | English: DAOD 7000-1, without its "(county, district, etc.)" part. French: from s. 41 of the Loi sur la preuve au Canada ("Déclaré devant moi à …, ce … jour de …"); the province clause is drafted. The French DAOD uses "ASSERMENTÉ OU AFFIRMÉ". |
| 13 | Alberta jurat, extra line | (official, see below) | Commissaire aux serments. / Commissaire aux serments à l'extérieur de l'Alberta. | French drafted; not confirmed in the French DAOD. |
| 14 | Caption under the declarant's signature | (declarant) | (déclarant) | Drafted. The French DAOD sample uses "(déposant)", the affidavit term. |
| 15 | British Columbia and Saskatchewan jurats, English | His Majesty's | (official, see below) | The DAOD still reads "Her Majesty's"; updated for the Crown at LCol Huet's direction. The French DAOD reads "Sa Majesté". |
| 16 | Worksheet certification above the claimant signature (not part of the declaration) | I certify that the information on this worksheet is correct and complete. | J'atteste que les renseignements figurant sur la présente feuille de travail sont exacts et complets. | Drafted for the tool. |
| 17 | Paragraphs 1 and 2, currency exchange made before the duty | 1. On \[date\], before leaving on temporary duty to \[destination\] to \[purpose\], I exchanged currency for use during that duty. 2. I exchanged \[amount sold\] for \[amount bought\] at \[bank or exchange office\]\[ and paid a service charge of \[amount\]\]. | 1. Le \[date\], avant de partir en service temporaire à \[destination\] pour \[objet\], j'ai échangé des devises pour les besoins de ce service. 2. J'ai échangé \[montant vendu\] contre \[montant acheté\] auprès de \[banque ou bureau de change\]\[ et j'ai payé des frais de service de \[montant\]\]. | Drafted 17 Oct 2026. Used when the exchange date is before the trip starts. Destination is the longest stay in the itinerary. |
| 18 | Paragraph 1, currency exchange made after the duty | On \[date\], after returning from temporary duty to \[destination\] to \[purpose\], I exchanged the currency left over from that duty. (Paragraph 2 as in item 17.) | Le \[date\], après mon retour de service temporaire à \[destination\] pour \[objet\], j'ai échangé les devises qui me restaient de ce service. (Paragraphe 2 comme à l'élément 17.) | Drafted 17 Oct 2026. Used when the exchange date is after the trip ends. |
| 19 | Member-edited facts, any declaration | (the member's own text for paragraphs 1 and 2) | (texte du membre pour les paragraphes 1 et 2) | Added 17 Oct 2026. The member can replace the built paragraphs 1 and 2 with their own wording; paragraph 3 (reason), paragraph 4 and the closing paragraph are unchanged. Edited wording is printed as typed. AJAG may wish to comment on whether members should be allowed to edit the facts, and whether any guidance text is needed. |
| 20 | Jurat date | this \[17th\] day of \[October\] A.D. 20\[26\] | ce \[17\] jour \[d'octobre\] 20\[26\] | Added 17 Oct 2026. The day, month and year fields are filled with the declaration date (today by default, editable, or left blank for the officer). The fixed jurat wording is unchanged. In French, "de" moves into the month field when filled so that elision is correct ("jour d'octobre"). |

This wording is used as published and needs no review.
