    For receipts marked missing. Format per DAOD 7000-1.

    Declarations to be generated

      Each missing receipt gets its own declaration, pre-filled from this claim. You declare it in person before an authorized officer, who completes the jurat. You both sign it, with a PKI certificate or by hand (set under Build the package).

      Declarant

      DAOD 7000-1 does not allow abbreviations of names, ranks, units or places. Spell them out in full.

      Jurat

      Officer taking the declaration (if known)

      Leave these blank and the officer can type them into the PDF before signing.
